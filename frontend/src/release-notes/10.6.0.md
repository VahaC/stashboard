## Highlights

Your dashboard is already responsive and phone-tested — but it wasn't an **app**, and its
notifications never reached the device actually in your hand. **10.6** closes both gaps and lets you
lay the dashboard out the way *you* think about your services.

- **Install Stashboard to your home screen** as a Progressive Web App — it opens like a native app,
  and the login/dashboard chrome even loads while you're offline.
- **Web push notifications** — get a notification on your phone or desktop the moment a service goes
  **down** or an update lands, even when Stashboard isn't open.
- **Custom card order** — drag your service cards (and your category groups) into exactly the order
  you want, and it sticks across every device.

## The details

### Install it as an app

Stashboard now ships a **web app manifest** and a **service worker**, so your browser offers to
**install** it. Once installed it runs in its own window with a proper app icon, and the app shell
(the layout, navigation and login screen) is cached — so opening it with no connection still boots
the interface. There's **no offline data sync**: your services, Docker and Proxmox data still load
when you're online. New releases update the installed app **silently** — no "reload to update"
nagging.

### Web push, as a first-class notification channel

Push joins email, Telegram and Apprise as a notification channel — and it reuses the **same** rules
you already know. The **same** events fan out to it:

- a service going **offline**,
- a **Docker image update** becoming available,
- **Proxmox package updates** pending,
- a **Proxmox node health alert**.

Each is **per-device**: you subscribe each browser/phone individually, and a notification goes to
every device you've subscribed. If one of your devices unsubscribes or expires, Stashboard notices
(the push service returns *gone*) and **quietly drops it** — no dead endpoints pile up. A transient
hiccup, by contrast, is retried on the next tick, and never blocks your email/Telegram/Apprise
delivery.

There's **nothing to configure on the server**: the signing keys (VAPID) are generated **once** on
first run and stored right next to your database, so they survive restarts and image updates
automatically — exactly like your encryption and JWT secrets.

### Order your cards your way

A new **Custom** sort mode adds a small **drag handle** (the grip icon) to each card. Grab the handle
and drop the card where you want it — with mouse **or** touch — while the rest of the card stays
clickable and the page still scrolls normally. Two orders are kept **independently**, so switching
**Group by category** on and off never scrambles your work:

- **Ungrouped:** one global card order.
- **Grouped by category:** an order *within* each group **plus** a draggable order of the **groups
  themselves** (drag a category header's grip handle). "Uncategorized" always sits last.

Your custom order is saved per user and **travels in Backup / Restore**, so it comes back exactly as
you left it on a fresh instance.

## Turning it on

**Installing + web push both require a secure connection.** Browsers only allow service workers and
push over **HTTPS** (or `http://localhost`). If you reach Stashboard over a plain `http://<LAN-IP>`
address, the browser won't offer install and the push section will tell you so — put Stashboard
behind a **reverse proxy with TLS** or a **Cloudflare Tunnel** first.

**Install the app:** in a supported browser (Chrome, Edge, or add-to-home-screen on mobile), use the
browser's **Install** / **Add to Home Screen** action in the address bar or menu.

**Enable web push (per device):**

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications → Web push notifications**.
2. Click **Subscribe this device** and **allow** the browser's notification prompt.
3. Use **Send test** to confirm it works. Your subscribed devices are listed there — remove any with
   the trash icon.

Repeat on each device you want notified. The channel counts as "on" for you as soon as **one** device
is subscribed — there's no separate switch.

**Route update/alert notifications to push:** offline (service-down) alerts go to push automatically
once you have a subscription. For **Docker update** and **Proxmox update / node-alert** notifications,
tick the new **web-push** toggle on the individual **Docker watch** or **Proxmox host** (right next to
the existing Telegram / Apprise toggles — it's disabled until you've subscribed a device).

**Use custom order:** on the dashboard, open the sort dropdown and pick **Custom order**. A grip
handle appears on each card — drag it to reorder (and, when grouping is on, drag the grip on a
category header to reorder groups). It saves as you go.

## Not included yet

This release is about the dashboard on your own devices. **Native app-store apps**, **offline data
caching / an offline write queue**, and **reordering the Docker or Proxmox pages** aren't part of
10.6 — the drag-and-drop order applies to dashboard service cards only.
